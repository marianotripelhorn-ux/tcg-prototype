Daggerlight faction artwork assets (experimental Gameplay v1)

The following 10 optimised WebP image files belong in this directory:

red-portrait.webp    red-banner.webp
blue-portrait.webp   blue-banner.webp
white-portrait.webp  white-banner.webp
saints-portrait.webp saints-banner.webp
ivory-portrait.webp  ivory-banner.webp

Source files: FACTION ART.zip from artist.
The 5 portrait files are 900x1200 and the 5 banners are 1600x320.
A dedicated PRI CUT image was not provided; ivory-banner.webp is cropped
from the original PRI.png.

Art binaries should be uploaded to the prototype/gameplay-v1 branch.
The build copies public/factions/* to the /gameplay-v1/factions/ URL.
